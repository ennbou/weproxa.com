WePROXA **3.9.0** makes captured data easier to explore and helps keep proxy sessions healthy while you work.

## New Features

- **JSON Tools** - format, browse, query, and derive schemas from JSON with tree, jq, and JSONPath views, including direct handoff from captured responses.
- **Network health monitoring** - watch for proxy, system proxy, local network, and connectivity issues with configurable in-app, desktop, and recovery alerts.

## Fixes & Improvements

- **Workspace navigation** - browse collapsible workspaces and manage scenarios through clearer inline actions, status indicators, and activation controls.
- **MCP plugin setup** - use a recommended cross-client installer or client-native commands with clearer readiness checks and setup guidance.

Thanks to everyone who shared feedback and helped make WePROXA better!

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there a trial?
The free tier is available forever with core features, so you can use Weproxa before deciding if you need Pro.

### How do I activate my license?
After purchasing, you'll receive a license key. Go to **Settings → License** in Weproxa and enter your key to activate.

### Can I use my license on multiple Macs?
Each license is for a single machine. Contact us if you need volume licensing.

### Do I get free updates?
Your **$25 Pro purchase** is a one-time payment and includes **1 year of updates**.

### Is Pro a subscription?
No. Weproxa Pro is a **one-time purchase**, not a recurring subscription.

### Is the payment secure?
Yes. All purchases are processed by **Lemon Squeezy**, a trusted merchant of record. Your payment information is handled securely and never stored on our servers.

### What are the free tier limits?
The free tier includes core tools with generous limits: up to 5 Map Local rules, 10 Block List rules, 2 Breakpoint rules, 3 Network Conditioning rules, and 5 SSL interception hosts. Pro removes all limits.

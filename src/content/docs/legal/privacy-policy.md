## Overview

WePROXA ("we", "our", "us") is a native macOS and Windows HTTP/HTTPS proxy debugger. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## Data That Stays on Your Device

WePROXA runs entirely on your local machine. **Network traffic you capture, inspect, or modify never leaves your computer.** Specifically:

- Captured HTTP/HTTPS requests and responses are stored locally in memory and in a local SQLite database.
- SSL certificates and private keys generated by WePROXA are stored in your local app data directory.
- Map Local rules, breakpoint rules, block list rules, and other tool configurations are saved locally as JSON files.

We have **no access** to your intercepted traffic or local configuration.

## Data We Collect

### Analytics (Umami)

We use [Umami](https://umami.is), a privacy-focused analytics service, to collect anonymous usage statistics on our documentation website (weproxa.com). Umami does not use cookies, does not track users across websites, and does not collect personal information. Data collected includes:

- Page views and referrer URLs
- Browser type and operating system
- Country (based on anonymized IP)

No personally identifiable information is collected by Umami.

### Error Tracking (Sentry)

The WePROXA desktop app may use [Sentry](https://sentry.io) for crash reporting and error tracking. When an error occurs, the following may be sent:

- Error message and stack trace
- App version and operating system version
- A randomly generated anonymous session ID

No captured network traffic, personal data, or file contents are ever included in error reports.

### License Validation

If you purchase a WePROXA Pro license, your license key is validated through [Lemon Squeezy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/). This involves:

- Sending your license key to Lemon Squeezy's API for activation and validation.
- Lemon Squeezy processes your payment and stores your purchase information under their own [privacy policy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/privacy).

We do not store your payment information. We only store your license key locally on your device.

### Desktop App Analytics and Feature Flags (PostHog)

The WePROXA desktop app may use [PostHog](https://posthog.com) for anonymous product analytics and feature-flag evaluation. Analytics is event-based: WePROXA records selected actions such as starting or stopping the proxy, opening or toggling a tool, managing a rule, applying a filter, changing a preference, or completing a license or update action.

Desktop analytics may include:

- A randomly generated anonymous app-install identifier and session identifier.
- App version, operating-system platform, locale, and license tier.
- Coarse event properties such as the tool or action type, counts, durations, enabled state, and categorized success or failure reason.

WePROXA does not identify people or accounts in PostHog and does not create person profiles. Automatic page views, autocapture, session recording, surveys, and performance capture are disabled in the desktop app. Raw errors are reduced to coarse categories before analytics events are sent.

Desktop analytics and feature-flag requests do **not** include intercepted request or response URLs, headers, bodies, cookies, local file paths, certificate material, license keys, or customer email addresses.

## Data We Do NOT Collect

- Your intercepted HTTP/HTTPS traffic
- Request or response bodies, headers, or URLs you debug
- Your SSL certificates or private keys
- Contents of files used in Map Local rules
- Your IP address (for app functionality)

## Third-Party Services

| Service | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Umami | Website analytics | Anonymous page views |
| Sentry | Crash reporting | Error details, app version |
| Lemon Squeezy | License & payments | License key, payment info |
| PostHog | Desktop app analytics and feature flags | Anonymous install/session IDs, selected usage events, and app/platform metadata |

Each service operates under its own privacy policy.

## Data Storage and Security

Captured traffic and local configuration are stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that store your captured traffic. Communication with third-party services (Sentry, Lemon Squeezy, PostHog) uses HTTPS encryption.

## Children's Privacy

WePROXA is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
